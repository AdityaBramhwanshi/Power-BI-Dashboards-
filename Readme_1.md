# Sales Performance & Revenue Insights Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
This project presents a client-ready Power BI dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance. 
The dashboard helps business stakeholders track revenue trends, identify top-selling products, and understand high-value regions.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance
- Identify revenue trends over time
- Analyze top-performing products
- Understand regional revenue contribution
- Support data-driven business decisions

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Total Quantity Sold
- Top Country Revenue

## Key Visuals
- Revenue Trend by Year & Month
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Revenue Contribution by Country
- Interactive Date and Country slicers

## Key Insights
- Revenue shows significant growth in later months, indicating seasonality
- A small set of products contributes a large share of total revenue
- United Kingdom is the highest revenue-generating market
- Average Order Value indicates strong per-order revenue potential

## Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory efforts on top-selling products
- Strengthen presence in high-performing regions
- Use seasonal trends to plan promotions and stock levels
- Improve customer retention to increase Average Order Value

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX
- CSV data cleaning and transformation
